Frequently Asked Questions

        

          
How is the EU Public Tenders Explorer data sourced?
All active call-for-tender counts and official notice samples are queried directly from the European Union Publications Office TED Search API v3 (api.ted.europa.eu/v3/notices/search), refreshed 2026-11-21, covering 10 major EU countries across 6 CPV sectors.

          
How can I connect EU TED tenders to Claude, Cursor, n8n, or my CRM?
Use our zero-dependency MIT MCP server (eu-us-opendata-mcp), our 9 free n8n workflow blueprints (official-opendata-mcp-n8n-templates), our hosted RapidAPI REST API (eu-ted-public-tenders-procurement-api), or our Pay-Per-Event Apify Actor (eu-public-tenders-ted-boamp).

          
What EU B2B compliance rules affect suppliers bidding on public contracts in 2026–2027?
Software and digital suppliers face EU Cyber Resilience Act Art. 14 reporting (in force since September 11, 2026 — automated via our CRA-SBOM-Guard GitHub Action) and French B2B Factur-X EN 16931 e-invoicing (reception active since September 1, 2026 — supported via FacturKit 2027 and our Factur-X GitHub Action).
